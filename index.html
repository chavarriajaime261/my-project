ALL INTEXAS #1
~9.4 AEW · Jul 12, 2025 Texas Death Match

JON MOXLEY vs. "HANGMAN" ADAM PAGE

AEW All In: Texas · Globe Life Field, Arlington, TX · AEW World Championship

Voters treat this as the emotional peak of the entire Death Riders era: a stadium main event where the violence — fork to the head inside the first minute, barbed wire, a bed of broken glass — never floated free of the story. Every weapon called back to a previous chapter of the Moxley–Page rivalry, and the crowd heat reads on tape like a title change people had waited a year for.

The finish, Page forcing Moxley to quit with the chain from their 2023 Texas Death match, is the kind of full-circle payoff the cagematch comment section usually argues about. Not here — the thread is nearly unanimous that this is AEW's best big-stage main event since Omega–Okada.

HOLLYWOODENDING #2
9.51 AEW · Mar 9, 2025 Falls Count Anywhere · No DQ

"TIMELESS" TONI STORM vs. MARIAH MAY

Hollywood Ending · AEW Revolution 2025 · Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles · AEW Women's World Championship

At 9.51, this sits among the five highest-rated women's matches in the entire cagematch database — and it earned that in Los Angeles, ten minutes from the Hollywood sign the angle was built on. A year-long mentor-betrayal story, told through a champagne bottle, a red shoe, and two performers who understood that a blood feud finale should feel like a film's third act.

Voters consistently single out the structure: it escalates like a deathmatch but every violent beat is character work. Meltzer went five stars; the fans went further. The comment thread reads like a wake for a rivalry nobody wanted to end.

WWEEVOLUTION #3
9.39 WWE · Jul 13, 2025 Women's World Championship

IYO SKY vs. RHEA RIPLEY

WWE Evolution 2025 · State Farm Arena, Atlanta

The highest-rated women's match in WWE history on cagematch — 9.39, cracking the database's all-time top 25 across every promotion and era. On the first all-women's WWE event in seven years, SKY and Ripley wrestled like they knew the night needed a historical anchor and simply built it themselves.

What the voters keep praising: the power-vs-flight structure never resets. Every Ripley counter of SKY's springboards gets countered back later, and the moonsault finish lands as the logical end of a forty-minute chess game compressed into twenty. A Money in the Bank cash-in attempt after the bell somehow didn't dent the score.

WRESTLEKINGDOM20 #4
~9.3 NJPW · Jan 4, 2026 Retirement match

HIROSHI TANAHASHI vs. KAZUCHIKA OKADA

NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 20 · Tokyo Dome · Tanahashi's final match

Twenty-six years of "The Ace," closed out by the rival who defined his last great decade. Okada — back in the Dome as a full heel — refused to give Tanahashi a sentimental farewell, pulling him up at two to inflict more damage before ending it with the Rainmaker at the 33-minute mark.

Cagematch reviewers called it "the best retirement match ever, absolutely flawless," and the vote pattern agrees: it's the top-rated match of Wrestle Kingdom 20 and an early frontrunner for 2026's match of the year. Tanahashi working in tributes to Nakamura and Shibata gave the thread its group-cry moment.

STEELCAGE #5
~9.3 AEW · Mar 9, 2025 Steel Cage

KYLE FLETCHER vs. WILL OSPREAY

AEW Revolution 2025 · Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

Mentor versus protégé inside a cage, on the same LA card as the Hollywood Ending — Revolution 2025 is the rare PPV with two matches in the year's top five. Fletcher came in as the Don Callis Family's chosen assassin; he left as a made man even in defeat, after a screwdriver, thumbtacks, and an avalanche Styles Clash that the comment thread still argues should have ended it.

Voters framed it as the night the "Aussie Arrow" became a main-eventer, and the rubber-match trilogy bout later in the year only pushed this one's rating higher in hindsight.

MONDAYNIGHTRAW #6
~9.2 WWE · Mar 3, 2025 TV match of the year

IYO SKY vs. RHEA RIPLEY

WWE Monday Night Raw · Women's World Championship title change

A free-TV title change that out-rated most of the year's pay-per-view matches. SKY winning the Women's World Championship on a random March Raw was 2025's biggest genuine shock, and voters rewarded a match built like a PLE main event: no commercial-break structure, no cooldown, just fifteen minutes of escalation.

On cagematch it became the yardstick every women's TV match of 2025 got measured against — and the reason the Evolution rematch four months later carried real big-fight weight.

OMEGAvsOKADA V #7
~9.1 AEW · Jul 12, 2025 Winner takes all

KENNY OMEGA vs. KAZUCHIKA OKADA

AEW All In: Texas · Unification match — AEW Unified Championship

Chapter five of the greatest rivalry of the modern era, eight years after Wrestle Kingdom 11, with Kota Ibushi and Don Callis in the corners and two belts becoming one. It couldn't be 2017 again — post-surgery Omega and veteran Okada wrestled a smarter, meaner match instead, built on eight years of muscle memory and counters to counters that only these two know exist.

The cagematch electorate scored it below the untouchable trilogy peaks but comfortably among 2025's elite — and the thread's consensus take is the right one: a legacy match that earned its place in the series instead of coasting on it.
