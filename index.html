About.

IPC — Instructions Per Clock — is a four-piece out of Los Angeles. We formed in a garage in 2022, wrote a lot of songs we threw away, and kept the ones that still sounded true at 3am.

The name’s a CPU metric. It measures how much actual work a machine does per tick of its clock. We think about it every time we’re on stage.
