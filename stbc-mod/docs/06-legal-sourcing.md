# 06 — Legal & Sourcing Notes

Short version: **keep it personal and non-commercial, respect each model's license, and
credit creators.** Fan mods like this are extremely common and generally tolerated, but
they live in a gray area — these notes keep you on the responsible side of it.

## The IP layers involved
There are *three* separate rights-holders to be mindful of:

1. **The game** — Star Trek: Bridge Commander (Star Trek IP). Modding for personal use is
   long-standing community practice.
2. **The foreign franchise** — Halo (Microsoft/343), Star Wars (Disney/Lucasfilm), etc.
   You're depicting *their* ships. Personal, non-commercial fan use only.
3. **The 3D model itself** — whoever made the mesh you download holds copyright on *that
   asset*, governed by its license. **This is the one you most directly must honor.**

## Practical rules
- **Non-commercial only.** Don't sell mods, don't put them behind paid walls, don't run ads
  on them. Never charge for anything containing someone else's IP or model.
- **Read each model's license before using it.** On Sketchfab/CGTrader/etc.:
  - **CC0** → free to use, no credit required (still nice to credit).
  - **CC-BY** → free to use **with attribution** (record the author + link).
  - **CC-BY-NC** → non-commercial + attribution (fine for us, still no selling).
  - **CC-BY-ND** → *no derivatives* — ⚠️ converting/editing may violate this; avoid for
    meshes you'll modify.
  - **"Editorial use only" / All rights reserved** → don't use.
- **Credit everyone.** Keep a `CREDITS.md` (or a credits block in each ship README) listing
  model author, source URL, and license for every asset.
- **Game rips are the murkiest.** Extracting a ship straight from Halo/Star Wars game files
  is itself infringing on the franchise's assets. Many people do it for personal mods, but
  prefer **community-made original models** with clear licenses where you can.
- **Distribution raises the stakes.** A mod on your own PC is low-risk. *Publishing* a mod
  containing others' IP/models is where takedowns happen — respect creators' wishes and
  any "do not redistribute" terms, and don't repackage others' converted ships without
  permission.

## A `CREDITS.md` entry template
```
## <Ship name>
- Model: "<title>" by <author> — <url>
- License: <e.g. CC-BY 4.0>
- Modifications: imported, rescaled, re-textured, exported to .nif for STBC
- Franchise: <Halo / Star Wars / …> © <rights-holder> (fan, non-commercial use)
```

## Bottom line
This toolkit assumes a **personal, non-commercial fan project**. Stay there, attribute
your sources, and you're aligned with how the BC modding community has operated for 20+
years.
